Coca-Cola made with American cane sugar

I bought this 6-pack bottle of Coca-Cola made with American cane sugar (at least 2 times) from QFC in the Seattle area. It comes with 6, 8-ounce bottles, and they're all made with American cane sugar, as opposed to high fructose corn syrup found elsewhere. (The Mexican version of that said soft drink also contains cane sugar and comes with a 12-ounce bottle.)

Upon tasting this version, it definitely does taste like the real thing! It's crisper, refreshing, and it tastes even better in a glass bottle (instead of a plastic botle or a can). I would definitely buy this again!

You can buy a 6-pack bottle of Coca-Cola made with American cane sugar everywhere you shop for a Coke

This product contains:
Carbonated water, cane sugar, caramel color, phosphoric acid, natural flavors, and caffeine.

RATING: INFINITY! out of 10

A 6-pack bottle of Coca-Cola, made with American cane sugar.

Mexican Coke

Mexican Coca-Cola is a very popular soft drink. Like the American version that comes with 6 bottles, this one is made with cane sugar instead of high fructose corn syrup found in other soft drinks, but unlike the American version, this comes with a thick, 12-ounce bottle. We have purchased this from Costco a couple of times as a fiesta pack. The fiesta pack comes with 12 bottles of Coke, 6 bottles of Fanta Orange, and 6 bottles of Sprite, all made with, again, cane sugar. It is crisp, delicious, and refreshing!

You can buy Mexican Coca-Cola everywhere you shop for a Coke!

This product contains:
Carbonated water, cane sugar, caramel color, phosphoric acid, natural flavors, and caffeine.

RATING: INFINITY! out of 10

Oreo-Flavored Coca-Cola

This was a limited-edition soft drink as part of the collaboration between Oreo and Coca-Cola, launched in fall 2024 through the Coca-Cola Creations range. I bought this from H-Mart in Bellevue on October 6th, 2024. When opening the drink, it smelled like it had some hints of chocolate, and once I drank it, it did exactly taste like Oreos, which was surprising.

Additionally, Oreo released its Coke-flavored cookies, which tasted exactly like Coca-Cola itself. However, I did not try that, and I am hoping Coke would release its another "Creations" lineup again.


According to the official Coca-Cola website, Oreo-Flavored Coca-Cola contains:
Carbonated water, caramel color, natural flavors, phosphoric acid, sodium benzoate (to protect taste), acesulfame potassium, sodium citrate, sodium chloride, aspartame, caffeine, and sucralose.

This product contains a source of phenylalanine.

RATING: INFINITY! out of 10

Pepsi "RAW?!" Cola

Do not be surprised if you see the word "RAW," because Pepsi now has its "raw-flavored" cola. Originated in Japan, it becomes available in China and Taiwan. I bought this from T&T Supermarket on December 2024, and although it did smelled like Pepsi, it was a bit too sweet to my taste (probably because it is zero sugar). It was like a watered-down version of this drink, and although it was OK, I would hope this would be improved in the future.

RATING (as of December 2024): 5 out of 10

Costco now has Coke!

In Summer 2025, Costco did the unthinkable: they switched to Coke products in their food courts nationwide. Traditionally, since 1983, Costco had been serving Coke products in their food courts, but by 2013, they switched to Pepsi products as a cost-effective way to keep their hot dog prices low at $1.50. Now, Costco has switched back to Coke products in their food courts, and I couldn't be more happier than that. Offerings from Coca-Cola include Coca-Cola Original Taste, Diet Coke, Coca-Cola Zero Sugar, Sprite, Minute Maid Lemonade, and one additional flavor (varies by regional) from The Coca-Cola Company. I remembered going to Costco in Tukwila after the switch and got our hot dog combos, all with Coke! It was indeed delicious!

Pepsi (from the Middle East)

It may be a bit difficult to find Pepsi Made with Real Sugar in a glass bottle recently, but I found Pepsi glass bottles imported from the Middle East (manufactured in Oman). I bought these from Lam's Seafood Market for $1.99 each on August 2026. It has a twist-off cap, but we always have a bottle opener, so I opened a bottle of Pepsi with that tool. At the time of this publishing, it is all written in Arabic (I'm assuming it's made with real sugar). However, the taste itself is smooth and refreshing (and not too sweet). I would buy this again, but whenever I find Pepsi Made with Real Sugar sold in 12-oz glass bottles in the future, then I will also buy this.

Pepsi from the Middle East contains:
Carbonated water, sugar, caramel color, phosphoric acid, gum arabic, natural flavors, and caffeine.


Pepsi made with real sugar (produced in the U.S.?) contains:
Carbonated water, sugar, caramel color, phosphoric acid, caffeine, and natural flavors.


RATING: INFINITY! out of 10

Pepsi Zero Sugar, Strawberry Milkshake Flavor from China

I bought this from T&T Supermarket on Sunday, August 23rd, 2026, for only $1.99 a can (very cheap, indeed!). This can has a pull tab in it (which does exist in the Middle East and China, but is nowadays uncommon in the U.S.), and it was so much fun pulling a tab. Unlike the Pepsi Draft Cola that I tried almost 2 years ago, this one tastes pretty decent. It has a creamy, strawberry milkshake flavor that is smooth and forwarded, something that I will buy this again! Pepsi Strawberry Milkshake Flavored Cola has surprised me a lot.

This soft drink contains:
Carbonated water, caramel color, phosphoric acid, pectin, sodium citrate, natural and artificial flavorings, aspartame, acesulmae potassium, caffeine, potassium sorbate (as preservative), sucralose, and sodium benzoate (again, as preservative).

This soft drink contains a source of phenylalanine.

Although it is strawberry milkshake flavored, it does not contain dairy (rest assured).


RATING: INFINITY! out of 10


Pepsi White Grapefruit and Bamboo Flavor

From T&T Supermarket Store in Bellevue, Washington for less than $2.10, Pepsi from China has offered this White Grapefruit and Bamboo flavored cola (the other offering is White Peach and Oolong flavored Pepsi, which I'll try it in the near future when I get a chance). The design looks very elegant and nice, with white grapefruit and bamboo all shown in a label. Upon opening it, it smells like regular Pepsi at first, but upon drinking it, it has the bamboo aftertaste, as well as some notes of grapefruit. It's more of like China's exclusive flavor that the U.S. does not have. I must say, this is a relaxing (and refreshing) drink!

This soft drink contains:
Carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, sugar, caramel color, phosphoric acid, caffeine, flavorings (one of the ingredients missing in the white label for imports, but should be there), low-calorie sweetners (acesulfame potassium, suclarose).


RATING: INFINITY! out of 10


Soda Hauls from Exotic Snack Guys

So, I was heard that Exotic Snack Guys has been officially opened inside The Outlet Collection Seattle Mall in Auburn, Washington since August 1st. The Exotic Snack Guys shop has many operations in the Midwestern and Eastern United States areas, so when they entered the West Coast for the first time, I was so hyped up for this store.

Therefore, on Sunday, September 13th, 2026, we went to The Outlet Collection Seattle Mall to buy some clothes and shoes from Nike and Adidas, respectively. Then, we stop by the Exotic Snack Guys store to buy three sodas. They are...

7up Salted Lemon Flavor from China

Inspired by a drink containing salted lemon in 7up!

Crush Blue Raspberry from Canada

A very popular drink in Canada, this flavor did exist in the U.S., under the name, "Back to the 80s: Electric Blue Razz Flavor." I'm pretty sure Crush brought back this flavor to Canada by a popular demand.

Crush Cream Soda from Canada

Exotic Snack Guys sells the clear version of Crush Cream Soda that is available in Quebec, as well as in Newfoundland and Labrador. There exists a pink-coloured version of this soda that is sold elsewhere in Canada
